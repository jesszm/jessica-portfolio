I'm a UI/UX designer with over five years of experience, passionate about digital experiences that are visually clear, functional and genuinely easy to use.

I've designed across e-commerce, mobile apps, web systems, fintech and software application interfaces. What ties it together is the same habit: understand the person on the other side of the screen first, then design the smallest interface that gets them what they came for.

I work in Figma daily and bring illustration skills from Adobe Illustrator and Procreate when a product needs a visual voice of its own. Communication and close collaboration with clients are essential to my process, so you'll always know what I'm designing, why, and what I need from you next.

How I work

Flow 03 · Prototype connections · Iterative

Discover

Align on the problem, the people, the constraints and what "success" will be measured by.

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • Brief & KPIs
  • Scope & risks

Research

Learn from real users and the market before drawing a single screen.

  • User interviews
  • Surveys & analytics
  • Competitive audit

Define

Turn findings into structure: who we're designing for and how the product is organised.

  • Personas / JTBD
  • Journey maps
  • IA & user flows

Design

From low-fidelity sketches to a high-fidelity UI backed by a component system.

  • Wireframes
  • UI & design system
  • Interactive prototype

Validate

Test with users, measure, and fix what matters most before anything gets built.

  • Usability testing
  • SUS · SEQ · task success
  • Prioritised iteration

Deliver

Hand off with specs, tokens and annotations, then follow the numbers after launch.

  • Dev handoff & QA
  • Design tokens
  • HEART metrics
Learn → iterate → ship again
