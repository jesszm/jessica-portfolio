About

nice to meet you

I design products end to end, from the first research question to files developers can build from without guessing.

I test before I polish and write every decision down, so the thinking survives the handoff and the next person on the file never starts from zero.

the one rule I design by“Good design is on your side,
never in your way.”

Two paths taught me what that means.

01

The eye

Shaped by creativity

My path began with colors and lines, in illustration, long before I opened Figma. That trained my eye for balance, rhythm and the small details people feel before they notice. That eye is what I bring to every interface: a hierarchy that guides attention, consistency across every state and screen, and the finish that makes a product feel trustworthy. When a brand calls for a visual language of its own and the scope allows it, I carry that craft into custom illustration and iconography.

In practice Visual hierarchy UI consistency Illustration, when in scope

02

The mindset

Guided by freedom

Freelancing turned the world into my office. Every new team brought a new rhythm and every culture a new way of seeing, so I learned to adapt fast, stay curious and design with empathy. It's why I get up to speed quickly in a new domain, from fintech rules to hospitality, and fit into the way a team already works instead of asking it to fit mine.

In practice Fast ramp-up Cross-cultural teams Async-friendly
